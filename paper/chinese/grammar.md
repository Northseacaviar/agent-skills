# Grammar — The Mechanics That Break

The errors below are the ones that survive a fluent-looking draft. Each has a test, not just a rule.

## 的 · 地 · 得

Formula: `X的 + 名词` · `X地 + 动词` · `动词/形容词得 + 补语`.

| Correct | Structure | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 高兴**的**孩子 | attributive + noun | A noun follows |
| 高兴**地**说 | adverbial + verb | A verb follows |
| 说**得**很高兴 | verb + complement | The thing after describes *how the action went* |
| 跑**得**快 | verb + complement | Not 跑地快 — 快 evaluates the running |
| 认真**地**工作 | adverbial + verb | Not 认真的工作 unless "the diligent work" is meant, which is a different sentence |

Native writers get this wrong constantly, so an error here does not read as foreign — it reads as careless. In a 简历, a 公文 or a 论文 that is a worse impression than a foreign accent.

Also: **strings of 的 are the most common ugliness in translated Chinese.** 我的朋友的姐姐的公司 → 我朋友姐姐的公司. Kinship and possession drop 的 freely (我妈, 我们公司, 你哥); only the last one in a chain needs it.

## 了 Is Not Past Tense

了 marks **completion or change of state**, and Chinese has two of them:

- **了₁** after the verb: the action is complete. 我吃了饭 (I have eaten).
- **了₂** at the end of the sentence: something has changed. 下雨了 (it has started raining — nothing past about it), 我知道了 (now I know), 十二点了 (it is now twelve).

Consequences that trip non-natives:

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 我昨天去了北京了 | 我昨天去北京了 | One 了 does the work; a time word already carries the past |
| 我以前是学生了 | 我以前是学生 | Habitual past takes no 了 |
| 我每天吃了早饭 | 我每天吃早饭 | Habitual action never takes 了 |
| 他很高了 | 他长高了 | 了 with an adjective needs the change to be real |
| 我在中国住了三年 | Both exist | 住了三年 = lived three years and stopped or is a bare fact; 住了三年了 = three years so far and continuing. The second 了 is the "still true" marker |

Negation kills 了: 没(有) + verb, with no 了. 我没吃了饭 is ungrammatical; 我没吃饭 is right.

## 把 and 被

**把** moves the object before the verb and requires something to happen to it. The verb cannot stand bare.

- 我把门关了 ✓ (关了 — a result)
- 我把门关 ✗ (nothing happened to the door yet)
- 把 requires a **definite** object: 把一本书拿来 is wrong; 把那本书拿来 is right.
- Use 把 when the sentence is about the fate of the object; otherwise 我关门了 is the neutral, more common form. Overusing 把 is its own non-native marker.

**被** carries adversity by default. This is the rule 欧化中文 broke:

| Sentence | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 他被骗了 | Natural — something bad happened |
| 他被表扬了 | Fine now; 被 with positive verbs has naturalised |
| 这个问题被讨论了 | Un-Chinese — use 这个问题讨论过了 or 大家讨论过这个问题 |
| 报告被完成了 | Wrong — Chinese uses the bare active: 报告写完了 |

Default: Chinese expresses the passive without a marker. 饭做好了, 会开完了, 问题解决了. Reach for 被 only when the agent matters or the event is adverse. 让 and 叫 are the colloquial passives (被 → 让/叫 in speech: 让他给弄坏了).

## 是…的

Marks that the *circumstances* of a completed event are what is being discussed — when, where, how, with whom.

- 我昨天来的 — the fact that I came is known; *yesterday* is the point.
- 我是坐飞机来的 — I came; the point is *by plane*.
- 我来了 — the point is that I came at all.

Non-natives under-use this and produce 我昨天来了 where a native asks 你什么时候来的 and answers 我昨天来的. Getting it right is one of the strongest fluency signals in ordinary conversation.

## Measure Words

个 always parses and always marks the writer as a learner. The high-frequency ones, with what they select:

| 量词 | Selects | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 位 | People, politely | 一位客人, 三位老师 |
| 条 | Long flexible things, and abstractly: messages, lives, roads, rules | 一条消息, 一条裤子, 一条命, 一条规定 |
| 张 | Flat things, and faces and tickets | 一张桌子, 一张票, 一张脸, 一张纸 |
| 部 | Films, phones, long works | 一部电影, 一部手机, 一部小说 |
| 台 | Machines | 一台电脑, 一台空调 |
| 家 | Businesses and institutions | 一家公司, 一家店, 一家医院 |
| 门 | Courses, disciplines, technologies | 一门课, 一门技术, 一门语言 |
| 顿 | Meals, and beatings and scoldings | 一顿饭, 骂了一顿 |
| 场 | Events with duration | 一场电影, 一场雨, 一场比赛 |
| 件 | Clothes (upper body), matters, luggage | 一件衣服, 一件事 |
| 双 / 副 | Pairs — 双 for symmetric pairs, 副 for sets | 一双鞋, 一副眼镜, 一副手套 |
| 只 | Animals, and one of a pair | 一只猫, 一只手 |
| 头 | Large livestock | 一头牛, 一头猪 |
| 棵 / 朵 / 片 | Trees / flowers / flat expanses | 一棵树, 一朵花, 一片叶子 |
| 份 | Portions, copies, jobs | 一份饭, 一份工作, 一份报告 |
| 遍 / 次 / 趟 | Times through / occurrences / round trips | 看了三遍, 去过两次, 跑一趟 |

The number two before a measure word is **两**, not 二 (`numbers-and-names.md`). 半 goes after the measure word for a half-unit: 一个半小时, not 一半个小时.

## Word Order

Chinese fixes the order that English varies. The canonical frame:

`主语 + 时间 + 地点 + 方式 + 动词 + 宾语`

我昨天在公司用微信跟他说了这件事。

- **Time before place**, always. 我在北京昨天见了他 is wrong.
- **Big before small** in time and place alike: 2026年7月26日下午三点, 中国北京市朝阳区.
- **Duration and frequency go after the verb**, and if there is an object the verb repeats or the object moves: 我学中文学了三年 / 我学了三年中文, never 我学中文三年.
- **Prepositional phrases go before the verb**: 跟他说, 给我打电话, 从家里出来. English's post-verbal "to him", "for me" moved in place is the most common word-order error.
- Topic-comment beats subject-predicate for anything already under discussion: 这件事我知道 (this thing, I know about it) is more natural than 我知道这件事 when 这件事 is the topic.

## Resultative and Directional Complements

English packs the result into the verb; Chinese attaches it.

| English | Chinese | Component |
|---|---|---|
| I found it | 我找**到**了 | 找 = look for; 到 = achieved |
| I understood (by listening) | 我听**懂**了 | 听 = listen; 懂 = understand |
| I finished reading | 我看**完**了 | 完 = complete |
| I could not see it | 我看不**见** | Potential form: 得/不 infixed |
| Bring it up here | 拿**上来** | Direction relative to the speaker |
| I can't afford it | 我买不**起** | 起 = the means to |

The potential infix is the piece learners skip: 看得懂 / 看不懂, 做得完 / 做不完, 吃得下 / 吃不下. It is far more common in speech than 能 + verb, and using 不能看懂 where a native says 看不懂 is immediately audible.

## Un-Englishing the Sentence

SKILL.md Rule 6 in its full form. Each of these adds characters and subtracts naturalness.

| Pattern | Fix | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 进行/作出/给予/予以 + noun | Use the verb | 进行讨论 → 讨论 · 作出决定 → 决定 · 给予帮助 → 帮助 |
| 一个 as an indefinite article | Delete | 这是一个很好的机会 → 这是很好的机会 (keep 一个 when the count matters) |
| 们 on an already-plural noun | Delete | 三个学生们 → 三个学生 |
| 性 / 化 suffix stacking | Rebuild | 可行性方案 → 可行的方案 · 进行了优化处理 → 优化了 |
| Long attributive before 的 | Split (SKILL.md Rule 5) | 我昨天在楼下咖啡店遇到的那个人 → 那个人，我昨天在楼下咖啡店遇到的 |
| 当…的时候 | 时, or nothing | 当我到家的时候 → 我到家时 / 到家一看 |
| 关于…方面 | Topic-first | 关于成本方面的问题 → 成本这块 |
| 因为…所以… both halves | Keep one | 因为下雨所以取消了 → 下雨，取消了 |
| 对…进行…的 chains | One verb | 对数据进行分析 → 分析数据 |
| 是…的 as a copula crutch | Delete | 这个方案是可行的 → 这个方案可行 |

余光中 and 思果 are the standard treatments of this pattern; the argument about how far to push it is in SKILL.md, Where Experts Disagree.

## Pairs That Get Confused

| Pair | Distinction |
|---|---|
| 会 / 能 / 可以 | 会 = learned ability or likelihood · 能 = capacity or circumstance permits · 可以 = permission |
| 一点 / 有点 | 一点 after an adjective and neutral (好一点) · 有点 before it and negative (有点贵) |
| 又 / 再 | 又 = repeated already · 再 = will repeat |
| 才 / 就 | 才 = later or harder than expected · 就 = sooner or easier |
| 还是 / 或者 | 还是 in questions · 或者 in statements |
| 二 / 两 | 两 before measure words · 二 in ordinals and compounds |
| 想 / 要 / 打算 | 想 = would like · 要 = will, or want insistently · 打算 = plan |
| 刚 / 刚才 | 刚 is an adverb before the verb · 刚才 is a time word before the subject or verb |
| 不 / 没 | 不 negates will, habit, future · 没 negates completion and possession |
| 常常 / 往往 | 常常 = frequently, any time · 往往 = tends to, only about patterns already observed |
| 帮 / 帮助 | 帮 colloquial and takes a following verb (帮我看看) · 帮助 formal and nominal |

## What Gets Written Down

Grammar itself is not user data, but two things this page produces are:

- **A construction the user repeatedly gets wrong**, or one a native reader flagged in their text → `### Corrections` in the glossary, with the wrong and right forms and who flagged it. A pattern corrected twice by the same reader belongs on paper, not in the session.
- **A house convention this page leaves open** — whether 首行缩进两字 is used, whether the user's field writes 数据 or 資料 — → the matching key in `config.yaml` under `conventions`, never in `memory.md` (`memory-template.md`).
