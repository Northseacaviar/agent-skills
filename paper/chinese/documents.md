# Formal Documents

**Before writing any document type the user has produced before**, read the `artifacts/` template `## Boxes` in `~/Clawic/data/chinese/memory.md` names for it. A 请假条 or a 辞职信 that was accepted unchanged is worth more than a fresh draft.

Formal Chinese documents are skeletons: the structure is fixed and the deviation is the message. Inventing a shape reads as not knowing there was one.

## 请假条

```
                            请假条
尊敬的<称呼>：
    您好。因<原因，一句>，需请<假别><N>天，时间自<YYYY年M月D日>至<YYYY年M月D日>。
    期间工作已交接给<姓名>，紧急事项可随时与我联系。恳请批准。

    此致
敬礼
                                                        申请人：<姓名>
                                                        <YYYY年M月D日>
```

- 假别: 事假 (personal, usually unpaid), 病假 (sick, attach the 证明 and say so), 年假 (annual), 婚假, 产假 / 陪产假, 丧假.
- 事假 states the reason in one clause and stops. Elaborating reads as excuse-making, which invites scrutiny.
- The handover sentence is what gets it approved. A 请假条 with no handover is a request the manager has to think about.
- Increasingly sent as a WeChat message rather than a document; the same four elements survive, minus the 此致 敬礼.

## 辞职信

```
尊敬的<称呼>：
    您好。因个人原因，本人决定辞去<职位>一职，最后工作日为<YYYY年M月D日>。
    在此期间我会完成手头工作的交接，确保平稳过渡。
    感谢公司这段时间的培养与支持。

    此致
敬礼
                                                        <姓名>
                                                        <YYYY年M月D日>
```

- **因个人原因 is the formula and it is doing deliberate work.** A resignation letter is a personnel file document, not a place to record a grievance. Whatever the real reason, it goes in the conversation, not the letter.
- Notice periods are contractual; the letter states the last working day rather than negotiating it.
- One sentence of thanks, sincere or not. Omitting it is legible as anger.
- 离职证明 (proof of separation) is a separate document the employer issues and the next employer will ask for.

## 感谢信 and 道歉信

Both are short, and both fail by being long.

- 感谢信: what was done (specifically, with the date), what it made possible, one sentence of thanks. Naming the specific act is the whole content; a general 非常感谢您的帮助 could have been sent to anyone.
- 道歉信: what happened, what the impact was, what has been done about it, then the apology, once. Apology-first reads as managing the reader's emotion instead of the problem (`etiquette.md`).
- Both take 此致 敬礼 when formal, 祝好 when semi-formal, and nothing when sent as an email body.

## 邀请函

Fixed fields: 被邀请人 · 活动名称 · 时间 · 地点 · 内容或议程 · 着装要求 (if any) · 回复方式与截止日期 · 主办方与联系人.

- 诚邀 / 谨定于 open the formal version: 谨定于2026年8月8日上午十时，于…举行…，诚邀您拨冗出席。
- 拨冗出席 and 莅临指导 are standard formal courtesies; 莅临指导 is for someone senior or external whose presence confers status.
- A visa invitation letter (邀请函 for 签证) has legally required content that varies by consulate — the format is not a language question, and getting it from the receiving authority is the first step.

## 简历

Chinese resume conventions differ enough from Western ones that a translated resume reads as a translated resume.

| Section | Convention |
|---|---|
| 个人信息 | Name, phone, email; photo still common on the mainland, less so in Taiwan. 籍贯 (place of origin), 政治面貌 (party status) and 婚姻状况 appear in state-sector and older-style resumes and are omitted in tech |
| 求职意向 | The target role, stated. A resume with no stated target is read as unfocused |
| 教育背景 | Reverse chronological; 985/211 and now 双一流 university tiers are shorthand recruiters read instantly |
| 工作经历 | Reverse chronological; company, role, dates, then achievements with numbers |
| 项目经历 | A separate section, expected in tech, often longer than 工作经历 for juniors |
| 技能 / 证书 | 英语六级 (CET-6), professional certifications, tools |
| 期望薪资 | Sometimes requested explicitly; 面议 is an acceptable answer |

- One page for under five years of experience, two beyond that.
- Achievements carry numbers with 万/亿 grouping (`numbers-and-names.md`): 负责的产品月活从3万增长到12万.
- Verbs: 负责 (responsible for), 主导 (led), 参与 (participated in), 推动 (drove), 搭建 (built). 参与 is honest and weak; using it where 主导 is true undersells, and the reverse is checked in interviews.

## 公文

Official Party and government document format is standardised (GB/T 9704, and the 2012 《党政机关公文处理工作条例》 which names fifteen document types: 决议、决定、命令、公报、公告、通告、意见、通知、通报、报告、请示、批复、议案、函、纪要).

The elements that matter when producing one:

- **标题** = 发文机关 + 事由 + 文种: 关于调整办公时间的通知. Never a title without the 文种 at the end.
- **主送机关** on its own line, ending with a colon.
- **正文**: 一、（一）、1.、（1） as the numbering hierarchy (`punctuation.md`).
- **落款**: issuing body and date, right-aligned, date written out in full.
- Typographic convention: 标题 in 二号小标宋, body in 三号仿宋, first-level headings in 黑体, second-level in 楷体. This matters when the document will be printed on official letterhead and is irrelevant in an email.
- Vocabulary: 兹 (hereby), 现将…通知如下, 特此通知, 请遵照执行, 妥否，请批示.

Nothing casual survives contact with this genre — no particles, no first person singular, no 成语 that is not established.

## 请示 and 报告 Are Different Documents

The single most common 公文 error, made by native speakers:

| | 请示 | 报告 |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Asks for approval or instruction | Informs, or answers a request |
| Requires a reply | Yes — the reply is a 批复 | No |
| Number of matters | One only | May cover several |
| Timing | Before acting | During or after |
| Closing | 妥否，请批示 / 当否，请指示 | 特此报告 |

**Never combine them.** A 请示报告 is a document that asks for approval while pretending to be informational, and it gets returned. If approval is needed, it is a 请示; if it is not, do not bury an ask inside a 报告.

## 合同 Language

Not legal advice — the vocabulary that makes a Chinese contract readable:

- 甲方 (party A, the commissioning side) and 乙方 (party B). Defined once at the top and used throughout.
- 本合同 / 本协议 for self-reference; 双方 for both parties.
- 应 = shall (obligation), 可 = may (option), 不得 = shall not (prohibition). These three carry the entire enforceability of the document and are never varied for style.
- 自…之日起生效 (effective from), 一式两份，双方各执一份 (two copies, one each), 未尽事宜，双方协商解决 (matters not covered to be resolved by consultation), 争议解决 (dispute resolution) naming the court or arbitration body.
- Amounts in both digits and anti-tampering numerals: 人民币壹万贰仟元整（¥12,000.00）(`numbers-and-names.md`).
- Dates written in full, no abbreviation.
- A contract touching liability, IP or employment is a lawyer's document; producing the Chinese wording is a language task, judging its effect is not.

## 证明 and 申请

- 证明 (certification letter — employment, income, residence): issuing body, subject's name and ID reference, the fact being certified, the date, the seal. The 公章 (official seal) is what makes it valid; an unsealed 证明 is a draft.
- 申请书 (application): 申请事由 → 个人情况 → 具体请求 → 承诺. Used for housing, subsidies, transfers, party membership.
- Both are read by an administrator matching fields against a checklist. Prose is not wanted; completeness is.
- An ID number (身份证号) inside a document is not written to `~/Clawic/data/` when the document is stored as a template (`memory-template.md`).

## Layout Conventions

- 首行缩进两字 (two full-width spaces of first-line indent) is standard in Chinese formal documents. Email increasingly uses blank lines instead; follow `conventions.paragraph`.
- The closing pair: 此致 either runs on after the last sentence of the body or sits on its own line 缩进两字, and 敬礼 goes on the next line 顶格 — flush to the left margin, never indented. The one thing that always reads as a mistake is 敬礼 indented further right than 此致. Some offices right-align both with the signature block; if the recipient's own documents do that, match theirs.
- Signature and date bottom-right, date in full Chinese form.
- Numbering hierarchy 一、（一）1.（1）, never the English 1. a. i. (`punctuation.md`).
- Titles centred; 主送机关 flush left.

## What Gets Written Down

- **Any document skeleton that was accepted unchanged** → `artifacts/template-<what>.md`, with the date, who accepted it, and any field that had to be adjusted. This is the highest-reuse artifact the domain produces and the reason `artifacts/` exists here.
- **The user's employer or institution's own conventions** — whether photos go on resumes, whether 政治面貌 is expected, which closing formula the office uses → `## Environment` in `memory.md`.
- **A document that was rejected or returned, and why** → `## Pain Points`, with the element that was wrong. A returned 请示 teaches more than a template.
- **Each document delivered** → a row in `pieces/<year>.md` with the type, the recipient and the outcome.
