## The Dating Rule

SKILL.md Rule 9, operationally:

1. **Can the era it peaked in be named?** If not, do not use it. A term whose date is unknown cannot be calibrated against an audience.
2. **Did it cross into ordinary vocabulary?** The observable test: mainstream press copy and job postings use it without quotation marks around it — 内卷, 打工人, 躺平 and 吐槽 all pass. If yes, it is safe indefinitely. If no, and it peaked more than roughly three years ago, it is period costume.
3. **Would this reader use it themselves?** Slang used *at* someone who does not use it reads as condescension or as a brand trying to be young — the most reliably embarrassing failure in this domain.

A dead meme dates the writer more precisely than plain language ever could. Plain writing has no expiry date; 然并卵 has a year stamped on it.

## Eras, So Terms Can Be Placed

Sample only, and deliberately weighted to older terms because those are the ones whose fate is already settled.

| Era | Terms | Status now |
|---|---|---|
| ~2010-2013 | 屌丝, 高富帅, 白富美, 土豪, 累觉不爱 | Dated; used ironically or by people who stopped updating |
| ~2014-2016 | 然并卵, 我伙呆, 城会玩, 蓝瘦香菇, 老司机 | Period costume; 老司机 partly survives |
| ~2017-2019 | skr, 真香, C位, 佛系, 锦鲤, 柠檬精 | 真香 and 佛系 survive as ordinary words; the rest are dated |
| ~2020-2021 | 打工人, 内卷, 凡尔赛, 破防, emo, yyds, 绝绝子 | 打工人, 内卷, 破防 crossed over; 绝绝子 was cringe within a year |
| ~2022-2024 | 躺平, 摆烂, 栓Q, 显眼包, 多巴胺穿搭, 松弛感, city不city | 躺平 and 摆烂 crossed over; the rest are still datable and fading |
| Current | Unknown from here | Ask the user what they have seen this month; do not guess |

The pattern worth extracting: **terms that name a social condition survive** (内卷, 躺平, 打工人); **terms that are only an intensifier or a joke die** (绝绝子, 栓Q, 蓝瘦香菇). When judging an unfamiliar new term, ask which kind it is.

## Pinyin-Initial Abbreviations

Formed from the first letters of each syllable's pinyin. Ubiquitous in chat and on 微博 and B站, invisible to anyone over roughly 40, and unusable in any formal context.

| Abbrev | Expansion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| yyds | 永远的神 | The GOAT, the best |
| xswl | 笑死我了 | Dying of laughter |
| u1s1 | 有一说一 | To be fair, honestly |
| nsdd | 你说得对 | You're right (often placating) |
| dbq | 对不起 | Sorry (light) |
| awsl | 啊我死了 | Overwhelmed, usually by something cute |
| zqsg | 真情实感 | Genuinely emotionally invested |
| bhys | 不好意思 | Excuse me / sorry (light) |
| xdm | 兄弟们 | Bros, lads |
| yysy | 有一说一 | Same as u1s1 |
| ssfd | 瑟瑟发抖 | Trembling (jokingly) |

Never in email, documents, or anything a manager reads. Fine in a peer group chat if `slang_appetite` is `current` or above, and if the reader already uses them.

## Numeric Slang

Homophone-based, older than the pinyin abbreviations, and understood far more widely:

- 666 — skilled, impressive (from 溜, smooth). Safe with most ages.
- 233 / 2333 — laughter, from an old forum emoticon index; the more 3s the harder the laugh.
- 520 / 5201314 — 我爱你 / 我爱你一生一世. Used in 红包 amounts (`chat.md`).
- 88 — bye (bye-bye), and 8 is lucky.
- 555 — crying (呜呜呜).
- 995 — 救救我.
- 484 — Taiwanese, 是不是.
- 4 is avoided in prices, floor numbers and gifts (homophone of 死).

## Audience Calibration

| Audience | `slang_appetite` | What that means |
|---|---|---|
| 50+, officials, clients, anything printed | none | No term that needs dating, including the crossed-over ones: swap the plain phrase in — 内卷 → 竞争过度, 躺平 → 不想再争, 干货 → 实用内容. No pinyin abbreviations, no 表情包 |
| Mixed-age workplace, external comms | light | Terms that crossed into vocabulary only (内卷, 躺平, 干货) |
| Peers under ~35, social copy, group chats | current | Datable current terms, pinyin abbreviations sparingly |
| Fan communities, B站, a personal account with an established voice | heavy | Community-specific vocabulary; getting it wrong here is more visible than not using it |

Sector matters as much as age: a 25-year-old in a state-owned enterprise writes closer to `none` than a 40-year-old on 小红书 does to `light`.

## Slang That Became Vocabulary

These no longer read as slang and are safe in ordinary writing, including some business writing:

内卷 (self-defeating competition) · 躺平 (opting out) · 摆烂 (deliberately not trying) · 打工人 (wage worker, self-deprecating) · 破防 (emotionally breached) · 真香 (eating one's words) · 佛系 (unbothered) · 干货 (substance) · 踩坑 / 避雷 (mistake / warning) · 平替 (cheap equivalent) · 社恐 / 社牛 (socially anxious / socially fearless) · 复盘 (retrospective) · 对齐 (align) · 反向 X (doing X in reverse).

Two of these carry an edge: 内卷 and 躺平 are commentary on working conditions, so using them upward in a company that recognises itself in them is a choice, not a neutral word.

## Where Slang Must Not Go

- 公文, 通知, contracts, 论文, 简历, legal or medical text — zero, at any `slang_appetite` (`documents.md`, `academic.md`).
- Anything addressed 您 (`register.md`).
- Anything that will be read after more than about a year: slang inside evergreen content dates the whole page.
- Anything that will be translated: slang is where machine translation fails hardest, and the translated version will be nonsense (`translate`).

## Crude Language

Governed by `crude_ok`, default false.

- 卧槽 / 我靠 / 我去 form a descending intensity ladder; 我去 is mild enough for mixed company, 卧槽 is not.
- 牛逼 and 傻逼 are common and crude; 牛 and 牛啊 are the clean forms of the first, and there is no clean form of the second.
- 尼玛, 特么, 麻辣隔壁 are euphemised swears, still recognisably swears.
- Regional swearing carries extra weight and travels badly; avoid entirely unless the user writes that way already.
- With `crude_ok: false`, do not silently substitute a mild form for a crude one the user asked for — say which word was avoided and why, once.

## Taiwan and Hong Kong Slang

Mainland slang does not carry across, and using it in Taiwanese or Hong Kong copy is as visible as the reverse (`regions.md`).

- Taiwan: 母湯 (don't do that), 傻眼貓咪, 484 (是不是), 顆顆 (the local 呵呵-equivalent, also dismissive), 484 and number play generally, 很盤 (overpriced), 阿災 (who knows).
- Hong Kong: written Cantonese carries its own register entirely — 好正, 唔該, 頂唔順, 咁都得. Writing Mandarin slang in Cantonese characters produces something nobody says.

## What Gets Written Down

- **A term judged stale for this audience, or adopted** → `### Retired Slang` with the term, the era it peaked, the verdict and the date. Without the peak year the verdict cannot be reviewed when the term returns ironically.
- **Terms the user's own audience actually uses**, observed in their messages or their comments → `### Terms`, marked as the audience's vocabulary. This is more reliable than any list on this page.
- **A slang term that landed badly** → `## Pain Points` with the reader's reaction.
- **The re-calibration cadence** → a `## Due` row, six months by default. This page is the strongest argument for that table existing at all.
