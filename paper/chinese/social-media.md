# Social Media — Platform Voices

**Before writing for a platform**, read the channel's `styles/<channel>.md` box if `## Boxes` in `~/Clawic/data/chinese/memory.md` names one, and its `## Channels` row if it does not. Every platform below is a different register with a different title mechanic; a post written in the wrong one reads as reposted from somewhere else, which is the fastest way to be scrolled past.

Platform limits and mechanics recorded 2026-07. The **register differences are stable**; the numbers move, so verify a limit before designing a format around it, and record what you find in `## Environment`.

## Pick the Platform First

| Platform | Register | Address | Post shape | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 小红书 | 日常, warm, first person | 姐妹们 / 家人们 / 你 | Short title + emoji-bulleted body + tags | Urban, skews female, 18-35 |
| 公众号 | 客气 to 正式, essayistic | 大家 / 你 | Title carries everything; short paragraphs | Subscribers who chose you |
| 微博 | 日常, fast, ironic | none, or @ | One paragraph, hashtags inline | Broad, public, argumentative |
| 抖音 / 快手 | 日常 to 亲近, spoken | 你 / 家人们 | One-line hook, caption supports video | Everyone, scroll speed measured in seconds |
| B站 | 亲近, in-group, meme-dense | 大家 / UP主 self-reference | Title as bait, 简介 as detail | Younger, ACG-literate |
| 知乎 | 客气, expository | 题主 / 大家 | Long-form answer, structure allowed | Readers looking for an argument |
| Else | 客气 | 大家 | Front-load the point, drop the scaffolding | Treat as 公众号 with a shorter attention budget |

## 小红书

- **Title**: about 20 characters, hard-truncated in the feed. Put the pain point in the first six. One emoji, at the start or the end, not both.
- **Body**: 1-3 sentences per paragraph, blank line between. Emoji used as bullets (✅ ❌ 💡 📍), not as decoration inside sentences. Around 1,000 characters is the ceiling for the note body.
- **Register**: 姐妹们 / 家人们 / 宝子们 as the address; first person, specific, confessional. A brand voice that stays corporate is invisible here.
- **Native vocabulary**: 种草 (making someone want it), 拔草 (buying it, or losing interest), 避雷 (warning), 踩坑 (the mistake you made), 干货 (substance, no fluff), 平替 (cheap equivalent), 保姆级 (exhaustively step-by-step), 无广 (not sponsored).
- **Tags**: 5-10, most specific first. A tag that names the exact product or the exact neighbourhood outperforms a category tag.
- **Ending**: one question to the reader. 你们还有什么推荐 is the standard and it works because comments are the ranking signal.
- Sponsored content must be declared (报备 through the platform's own mechanism); an undeclared ad is a takedown risk, not a style choice.

## 公众号

- **The title is the whole game.** Around 64 characters is the field limit; the first 20 or so are what shows in the subscription list. Two-part titles split by ｜ or ， are the dominant pattern: 别再背单词了｜三个月托福从80到105.
- **摘要** (the preview line) is separate from the first paragraph. Left empty, the platform takes the opening sentence — so the opening sentence must survive being read alone.
- **Paragraphs are one to three sentences**, with a blank line between, because the whole thing is read on a phone. A five-line paragraph is a wall.
- **自称**: 小编 is the traditional public-account self-reference and now reads as slightly dated and corporate; 我 reads as a person. Pick one per account and record it in the style box.
- **Closing**: 点个「在看」 / 转发给需要的朋友 / 关注不迷路 — one call to action, not three.
- The editor is not markdown. Bold, colour and dividers are applied in the platform editor, and pasted markdown arrives as literal asterisks — a fact worth recording in `## Environment` the first time it bites.

## 微博

- The timeline shows roughly the first 140 characters and then 展开全文. Long posts are allowed; the first 140 characters still have to work alone.
- Hashtags are **double-hash and inline**: #话题名# placed at the start or woven into the sentence, not appended as a list.
- Register is fast and ironic. Long unbroken sincerity reads as an advertisement.
- 超话 (super topics) are the fandom sub-communities; posting into one uses that community's vocabulary or gets ignored.
- Reposting with a comment (转发并评论) is the main circulation mechanic — write posts that give the reposter something to say.

## 抖音 and 快手

- The caption supports the video; it never carries the content. First line is the hook and it is what appears over the video.
- Spoken register throughout: this text will be read aloud in the viewer's head, so it follows `speaking.md` rules — short clauses, topic repeated, no 书面语.
- 完播率 (completion rate) drives distribution, so the hook names the payoff: 三步搞定，最后一步90%的人做错.
- 家人们 / 老铁 (快手 register, warmer and more northern) / 宝子们 (抖音, younger).
- On-screen text is part of the copy. Keep it to a few characters per line — Chinese characters are dense and a full sentence over video is unreadable at phone size.

## B站

- Title is bait, 简介 is the honest description, and the audience punishes a mismatch in the comments harder than the algorithm does.
- 三连 (点赞、投币、收藏) is the ask. 一键三连 is the standard phrasing.
- Vocabulary: UP主 (creator), 阿婆主 (playful variant), 弹幕 (scrolling comments), 前排 (early comment), 空降 (jumping to a timestamp), 补档 (reposting deleted content).
- 弹幕 culture means the video is watched with commentary over it: leaving a deliberate gap for a joke is a craft here.

## 知乎

- 谢邀 (thanks for the invitation) is now used ironically as often as sincerely; opening with it and then answering a question nobody invited reads as a joke about the platform.
- 利益相关 (disclosure of interest) at the top is expected when the answer touches the writer's employer or product. Omitting it and being found out is the main reputational failure here.
- Structure is welcome — this is the one platform where 一、二、三 headings do not read as AI scaffolding, because the format is expository by convention.
- 以上 as a closing line is standard and native on 知乎, unlike in email.

## Titles That Work in Chinese

| Pattern | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Number + payoff | 三个方法，通勤时间少30分钟 | Specific, countable, promises a bound |
| Negation of a common belief | 别再背单词了 | Contradiction earns the click |
| Pain point in the first six characters | 通勤太累？ | Survives truncation |
| Question the reader has asked themselves | 为什么你的PPT总是很土 | Second person, uncomfortable |
| Before → after with numbers | 从80到105，三个月 | Concrete transformation |
| 避雷 / 踩坑 / 保姆级 / 干货 | 保姆级教程：第一次报税 | Platform-native vocabulary signals the genre |

Do not: a title that is a complete formal sentence with 。at the end; a title starting with 关于 (that is 公文, `documents.md`); a title whose meaning depends on an emoji that some clients strip.

## Wording That Gets a Post Limited

Mainland platforms filter, rate-limit or shadow-restrict posts on wording, and the rules are neither published nor stable. When `platform_filter_aware` is true:

- Flag the risky wording rather than silently rewriting it, and offer the alternative — the user decides what their account can afford.
- The common substitution mechanic is 谐音 or character splitting: 钱 → 米, 死 → 4, 微信 → 威信 / v信, 加微信 → 加v. Native writers use these constantly and readers decode them without effort.
- Categories that most often trip filters: money and payment solicitation, health and medical claims, competitor platform names, links to off-platform destinations, and anything reading as political commentary.
- Absolute superlatives (最, 第一, 国家级, 顶级) in commercial copy are restricted by mainland advertising law, not only by platform policy — 最好用 in an ad is a legal exposure. This is the one item on this list with a statutory basis rather than a platform rule.
- A post that was limited is an `## Environment` fact worth recording with the exact wording, because the same phrase will be reached for again.

## What Gets Written Down

- **A channel's voice once it outgrows one line** — register, address, title mechanic, emoji density, opener and closer, what is banned → `styles/<channel>.md`, with its `## Boxes` line in the same turn. This is the box that makes two posts a month apart sound like the same account.
- **Every piece published** → a row in `pieces/<year>.md` with the channel, the audience, the length and **how it landed** (views, comments, whether it was limited). Without the last column the log is a diary.
- **A title that outperformed, or a formula that repeatedly works** → `artifacts/headline-bank-<channel>.md`, appended to rather than recreated.
- **A platform behaviour that cost effort to discover** — an editor that strips markdown, a field that truncates earlier than documented, a word that got a post limited → `## Environment`.
- **A cadence for re-checking platform rules** → a `## Due` row with `on change` as the interval.
