## Description:

Writes and edits Mandarin Chinese so messages, documents, social posts, names, and formal text read naturally for the intended audience, register, and regional variant.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and creators use this skill to draft, revise, and localize original Mandarin Chinese writing across casual chat, business email, social media, academic text, documents, speeches, names, and proofreading workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persistently record people, channels, templates, work context, writing history, and outcomes in local shared notes.

Mitigation: Review files under ~/Clawic/data/ before use with private workplace, client, HR, legal, or personal documents, and manually control or disable memory writes when sensitive context should not be retained.

Risk: Register, regional variant, slang, or address-form choices can create reputational or relationship risk if reused in the wrong audience context.

Mitigation: Review generated Chinese before sending, especially for workplace, customer, formal, legal, academic, or public social media use.
